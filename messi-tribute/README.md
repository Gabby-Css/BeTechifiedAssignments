# Messi – "The Last Tango" tribute video (9:16, ~43 s)

A vertical short for TikTok / Reels / YouTube Shorts about Lionel Messi's farewell from the
Argentina national team. **Everything in it is generated by code** (visuals, music, sound effects),
so there is no third-party footage or music that can be muted, blocked or struck.

`out/messi_tribute.mp4` – the finished video (1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC, ≈ -14 LUFS)
`out/cover.png` – a cover frame for the thumbnail
`make_video.py` – the whole pipeline in one file

## Run it

```bash
pip install pillow numpy          # Python 3.9+; FFmpeg must be on PATH
python3 make_video.py             # full render  -> out/messi_tribute.mp4  (~2-4 min on 4 cores)
python3 make_video.py --still 2.9 cover.png   # preview one frame at 2.9 s
python3 make_video.py --audio-only            # just the synthesized score (out/score.wav)
```

Fonts: uses [Inter](https://rsms.me/inter/) (free, SIL OFL) if installed, otherwise falls back to
Arial Black / DejaVu Bold. Edit the text in `scenes()`; edit timing with the beat helper `b(n)`
(90 BPM, 16 bars = 42.67 s). Hit times live in `HITS` and drive **both** the screen flashes/shakes and the
audio impacts, so picture and sound stay in sync when you move things.

### Putting real footage underneath (only footage you're allowed to use)

```bash
python3 make_video.py --clips ./my_clips      # .mp4/.mov/.webm videos and .jpg/.png photos
```

One clip per bar (2.67 s) is cut under the graphics, darkened so the text stays readable. Photos get a slow
Ken-Burns zoom. **Only use media you own, have a licence for, or have written permission to use.**
Good, legitimate sources:

* your own phone footage from a watch party / stadium;
* official press kits and channels that explicitly allow reuse (check each one's terms);
* creators who say yes when you message them (get it in writing and credit them in the caption);
* TikTok's own **Stitch / Duet** buttons – they keep the original creator credited and are the
  platform-approved way to react to somebody else's clip;
* CC-licensed photos on Wikimedia Commons (follow the attribution rules in each licence).

Downloading other people's clips from TikTok/YouTube and re-uploading them is the fastest way to get a video muted,
demonetised or your account struck – so the script doesn't do that.

## Posting checklist (suggestions, not guarantees – nobody can promise views)

1. **Cover frame:** use `out/cover.png`; TikTok lets you pick the cover from the video or upload a still.
2. **Caption idea:** *"He didn't retire from football. He said goodbye to Argentina. 208 caps. 126 goals. Gracias, Leo."*
3. **Hashtags (a few, not 30):** `#Messi #Argentina #LastTango #GraciasLeo #Football`
4. **Sound:** add a trending track from TikTok's own sound library at low volume under ours, or swap ours out – this
   is the one thing that can boost reach and is only available inside the app.
5. **Post early after the news, reply to comments in the first hour, and ask the question on screen** ("Comment your
   favorite Messi moment") – comments are a strong engagement signal.
6. **A/B test:** post a second version with a different first line (e.g. "208 CAPS. 126 GOALS." as the hook).
7. Pin your best comment, and don't forget to credit anyone whose clips you add.
